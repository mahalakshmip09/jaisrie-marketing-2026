About Jaisrie Marketing

Who We Are

A Sivakasi-rooted team powering South Tamil Nadu's solar transition

Jaisrie Marketing is a Solar Company headquartered in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu. We design and install rooftop, ground-mount and industrial-scale solar systems for homes, businesses and factories across South Tamil Nadu.

What began as a small local installation team has grown into a full-service solar partner — handling everything from the first site survey to subsidy paperwork, installation, testing and years of after-sales support. We work with Tier-1 panel and inverter brands and train our own technicians to the highest safety standards.

Whether it's a single rooftop for a family home or a multi-megawatt system for an industrial plant, our approach stays the same: honest sizing, transparent pricing and installations built to perform for 27 years.

Our Mission

To make clean, reliable solar energy accessible and affordable for every home, business and industry in South Tamil Nadu — through honest advice, quality installation and dependable after-sales support.

Our Vision

To be South Tamil Nadu's most trusted solar EPC partner, recognised for the quality of our installations and the strength of our long-term customer relationships.
